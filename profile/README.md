### 👋 Who we are

**Active Bridge** gives you dedicated full-stack developers who join your in-house team and work like they were always part of it. We're headquartered in **Arizona, US**, and our R&D center is in **Ukraine**. We started in 2013 as a small team of Ukrainian developers who loved Ruby on Rails. Since then we've built and maintained websites, web platforms, mobile apps and software products for clients around the world.

> We're not just coders, we're product strategists. We work in Agile, follow UX best practices and run a development process we've refined for over a decade, so we build products people actually enjoy using.

### 💜 Why teams work with us

| | |
|---|---|
| 🧠 **Technology expertise** | You get the full range of our team's skills. About 90% of our developers are senior or middle-level Rails engineers with deep object-oriented experience. |
| 🔥 **A motivated team** | Our remote developers have years of experience inside client teams and treat every project like their own. New tech, hard problems and open source keep them going. |
| 🔍 **Transparent workflow** | We join as many meetings as you need and send daily, weekly or monthly reports. Each developer works on **one project only**, so you get their full attention. |
| 🔄 **Agile by default** | Add experienced developers project by project. They join like in-house staff and follow Scrum or Kanban. |
| 🤝 **IT staffing** | We find and vet candidates from our bench with the exact skills and seniority the role needs. |
